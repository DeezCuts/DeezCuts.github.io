Meet D.

Duron Hill — known to everyone as D — runs a one-chair shop in Arden where the craft comes first and every client feels like family. He built Deez Cut's on a simple idea: treat every person who sits down with real care and real skill.

Clean shop. Sanitized equipment. Good conversation. Whether it's your first visit or your fiftieth, you'll leave looking right and feeling like you belong there.

With over 40 five-star reviews and a loyal clientele across WNC, D has built something rare: a barbershop people actually talk about.
